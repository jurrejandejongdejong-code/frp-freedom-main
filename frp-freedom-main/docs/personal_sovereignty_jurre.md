Personal Sovereignty Framework — Jurre Jan de Jong
Document timestamp: 2026-06-23

Core principle
- This is an inner, personal tool for empowerment, boundary-setting, and wellbeing. It applies only to your internal experience and personal timeline, not to shared external reality.

Protection statement
- Your memories, choices, and future belong to you. This framework supports your autonomy and psychological safety. It does not claim authority over others or the external world.

Sanctuary & border protocol
- Inner Sanctuary: a mental and emotional space where you are the sole steward.
- Border Protocol:
  - Awareness: notice what is yours and what is external.
  - Boundary: deliberately separate internal processing from outside input.
  - Release: let go of thoughts or energies that harm your wellbeing.
  - Protect: use self-care and practical action to maintain stability.

Inner resources (symbolic)
- 144,000: a symbolic representation of multiplied inner strength and resilience.
- Elyndor: a symbol of self-healing, renewal, and compassion.

Usage guidelines (safe)
- Use this for self-empowerment, not to claim power over others.
- Maintain real-world responsibilities; do not substitute inner ritual for necessary action.
- Respect others’ autonomy and beliefs.

Daily practice (brief)
- Morning: acknowledge your timeline as your own.
- Throughout the day: check boundaries; pause and breathe when triggered.
- Evening: integrate lessons, release what no longer serves.

Emergency grounding (brief)
- Stop. Breathe: 4-4-4 (inhale 4s, hold 4s, exhale 4s) three times.
- Name five things you see, four you feel, three you hear, two you smell, one you taste or one calming thought.
- Re-center: "This is my inner space. I am safe. I will act from care."

Declaration (concise)
- This tool was created for Jurre Jan de Jong on 2026-06-23 for private inner use. It respects others and does not assert claims about external reality.
