Emergency Grounding Script for Jurre Jan de Jong

When you feel overwhelmed or disconnected, use this short grounding practice.

1. Stop and breathe.
- Inhale slowly for 4 seconds.
- Hold for 4 seconds.
- Exhale for 4 seconds.
- Repeat this cycle three times.

2. Bring your attention to the present.
- Name five things you can see.
- Name four things you can feel.
- Name three things you can hear.
- Name two things you can smell.
- Name one thing you can taste or one calming thought.

3. Re-center yourself.
- Say quietly: "This is my inner space. I am safe. I will act from care."
- Choose one small, practical step to move forward.

Use this script as a gentle reminder that your inner sanctuary is there to support you, and that you can return to grounding whenever you need it.
